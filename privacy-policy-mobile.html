Privacy Policy for Selfie Worm (Mobile)

Last Updated: October 2026

Straight Banana ("we", "our", or "us") developed Selfie Worm as an ad-supported game with optional in-app purchases. This Privacy Policy informs players regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when playing Selfie Worm on mobile devices (Android and iOS).

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not operate proprietary game servers or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number.

However, to provide core gameplay functionality, monitor performance, and deliver ads, the game integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect certain automated data:

A. Analytics (Unity Analytics)

We use Unity Analytics (provided by Unity Technologies) to understand how players interact with the game and improve stability. Unity Analytics may collect:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device/installation identifiers, and language settings.
  • Gameplay Events: Session duration, level progression, in-game achievements, and diagnostic/crash data.

B. Advertising (Ad Networks)

The free version of the game displays ads (e.g., interstitial ads, rewarded video ads). Our third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads) may collect and process:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA / IDFV).
  • Network Data: IP address (used to infer coarse/general geographic location) and network status.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad impressions, clicks, and completion views for fraud prevention and performance measurement.

C. In-App Purchases (Unity IAP)

If you purchase the full-version unlock or other digital content, transactions are processed directly by Google Play (Google LLC) or the Apple App Store (Apple Inc.). We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers or financial billing information.

D. Cloud Saves & Game Services

The game supports optional platform sign-in via Google Play Games Services (on Android) or Apple Game Center (on iOS) to sync achievements and cloud save files. This data is handled according to your account settings with Google or Apple.

2. Third-Party Privacy Policies

For more detailed information regarding how our third-party service providers handle your data, please review their respective privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics & Unity Ads)
  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob
  • Apple Privacy Policy

3. Photo & Social Sharing Feature

Selfie Worm includes an in-game selfie and photo feature. Any exported photos or memes are saved locally to your device or shared via your device's native sharing sheet (e.g., Instagram, Threads, LINE) only upon your direct action. We do not transmit or store your exported photos on any external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Selfie Worm does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information through a third-party service, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. Data Deletion and User Rights

Because we do not store personal account records on our own servers, you can clear all local saves, cached files, and preferences directly by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the game. For data collected by third-party analytics or ad networks, you may exercise your opt-out rights via your device's privacy settings (e.g., "Reset Advertising ID" or "Delete Advertising ID").

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
