Privacy Policy for Selfie Worm

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handles user information and data when you play Selfie Worm.

1. Information Collection and Usage

We respect player privacy. We do not collect, track, or store any personal data, analytics, or telemetry on our own servers.

Steam Community & Workshop Features

The Game includes opt-in community features powered by the Steamworks API (provided by Valve Corporation):

  • In-Game Photos and Screenshots: If you choose to enable the photo-sharing feature, images captured within the Game will be uploaded directly to your personal Steam Workshop / Community Profile.
  • Steam Integration: The Game interacts with the Steamworks API solely to facilitate Workshop uploads and Steam Achievements. We do not store your Steam login credentials, personal profile data, or captured images on external servers.

2. Your Control and Choices

  • Opt-In Uploads: Photo-sharing to the Steam Workshop is entirely optional and requires your explicit consent in-game.
  • Content Management: You retain full control over your uploaded photos and can manage, change visibility settings for, or delete your content at any time directly through your Steam Account Profile.

3. Third-Party Services

This Game utilizes third-party platform services provided by Valve Corporation:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam / Steamworks): Steam Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Language Disclaimer

This Privacy Policy is provided in English. If this policy is translated into another language, the English version shall prevail in the event of any conflict or inconsistency.
